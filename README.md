# NComm_DSB_Heat

R codes for the SK-DSB project entitled "Exposure to ambient heat and hospital admission risks in people with disabilities: a nationwide time-stratified case-crossover study in South Korea"
: Published at Nature Communication
: Estimate the heat risk among people with disabilities by disability type and compare the risk with people without disabilities

Computational Environment: R version 4.2.3
R packages required: dlnm; lubridate; tsModel; survival
